Informed Consent

This study investigates how different recommendation approaches affect your experience with a group food recommender system. It is conducted as part of a bachelor's thesis at the University of Regensburg.

What will happen: You will chat with a recommender system, receive recipe suggestions for a virtual group, and answer a short questionnaire — three times in a row. The whole session takes approximately 10–15 minutes.

Your participation is voluntary and fully anonymous. No personal data beyond age and gender is collected. You may close the browser at any time without consequence. Data is used solely for research purposes.

If you have questions, contact: Maximilian.Schnabel@stud.uni-regensburg.de
